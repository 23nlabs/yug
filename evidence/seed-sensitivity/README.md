# Yug-1 sampling-seed figure

This figure is from 23N AI Labs' *Mithai Shop Challenge* v3 review draft. It visualizes regret across 16 synthetic retail experiment comparisons for five sampling seeds (100 paths each). Seed 67067, used in the earlier v1/v2 analyses, was the most favorable in 15 of the 16 comparisons.

**Scope:** simulated Ahmedabad retail demand only; no real retailer transactions. This figure does not establish real-world effects or prove why the seed sensitivity occurs.
